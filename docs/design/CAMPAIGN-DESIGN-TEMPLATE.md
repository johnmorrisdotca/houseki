# Campaign design template

Copy this worksheet for a game's next campaign revision. Fill it from actual rules, measured examples and reviewed family precedents; do not copy another game's weights without testing them. The process is in [CAMPAIGN-REVIEW-WORKFLOW.md](CAMPAIGN-REVIEW-WORKFLOW.md).

## Measurement contract

- Which decisions can change a later outcome:
- How forced and consequential choices are distinguished:
- Complete-plan sampling policy, sample size, seed stream and horizon:
- Exhaustive or bounded search policy and incomplete-result representation:
- Witness length, shortest-plan bounds and necessary setup measures:
- Interaction, target coordination, space and budget-slack measures:
- Mechanic-specific counterfactuals:
- Exact score formula, feature scales, weights and comparison categories:
- Integer 1–100 presentation and five-mark conversion:
- Technique/band admission evidence independent of display position:
- Human calibration status and remaining review:

Do not substitute first-step probes for whole-plan sampling. Random success compounded over independent easy tasks is not evidence of interacting expert planning.

## Revision findings

For each correction record the failed assumption, a concrete example, the changed construction or measurement, and its regression test. Carry general lessons back into the shared review workflow. Record published outcomes separately from proposed next steps.
